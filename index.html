The CRM Migration Record

Raiser’s Edge to CiviCRM to Zoho, 2015–2026

How the Libertarian National Committee moved its member and donor database off Raiser’s Edge, built and then abandoned CiviCRM, moved to Zoho, and sold the CiviCRM system to Ken Moellman. Each step shows who acted, what the record says in their words, and where the record goes silent.

Every quotation from the minutes was checked word for word against the cited PDF page. Quotations marked Recording come from YouTube auto-captions and were checked against the transcript at the time given; captions can mishear words. Payment totals are from the LNC’s FEC filings. The 2025–26 contract and breach quotations come from the board’s released breach materials, checked in the project’s 2025–2026 history.

Feb 20, 2016
Key findingSam Goldstein

Raiser’s Edge Won’t Release Pledgers’ Card Data

“Raiser’s Edge will not provide us the donor credit card information for our monthly pledgers to facilitate us transferring them to another system.”Minutes, Feb 20 2016, p.7

Monthly pledges could not simply be carried over to a new system. When the switch finally came in 2023, recurring revenue held in Raiser’s Edge was lost. Moellman was not at this meeting attendance, p.1; his first LNC business-list post is June 1, 2016.

Phase Three

The LNC orders a study of what CiviCRM can’t do. The study never reaches the record, and the LNC votes to migrate anyway.

The shared “IS Committee” Zoho account downloads full CRM backups repeatedly and exports about 200,000 contact records on Jan 8, 2026. Counsel attributes the exports to Kyle Davis. On a call, Davis describes this as something still to come:

Where The Record Goes Silent

Gaps & Missing Documents

G1

The Gap Analysis Report

Ordered Dec 2021, due by the March 2022 meeting, cited by Burns in July 2022. Never found.

Searched: LNC minutes Dec 2021–Jul 2022; all seven 2022 ExCom minutes before Jul 31; the LNC business list; meeting recordings; the Wayback Machine’s copies of 2021–22 lp.org uploads; LPedia’s meeting pages.

G2

The March 2022 LNC Meeting

Named as the report’s deadline, but never held. LPedia’s meeting list goes from Jan 23 to Apr 18, 2022.

G3

IS Committee Records, 2021–22

The committee’s own minutes, where the analysis would have been worked on, are not published. Its chair was Liz DelSignore; its secretary was Ryan Graham.

G4

The Speaker On July 31, 2022

The captions don’t name who said “we never met again.” The recording can settle it.

G5

The Zoho Decision

Announced in February 2024 as a “decision”; no vote, contract or price appears in the minutes.

G6

The 2024 Hosting Agreement

Payments to Moellman begin in January 2025, but the agreement is absent from the minutes.

G7

Approval Of The 2025 Sale

No LNC or ExCom vote approving the April 2025 transfer was found.

G8

Zoho Data In Moellman’s System

Whether any exported member data went into the CiviCRM Moellman owned, and on whose authority, is unrecorded either way.

IT Committee chair, 2014–2016

IT chair · lead consultant · Vice-Chair · owner

IT Committee chair 2016–17, CRM lead consultant by 2018, committee member again from 2019, mover of the gap analysis in 2021, volunteer through 2023, and owner of the CiviCRM system from April 2025.
