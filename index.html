The CRM Migration Record

Raiser’s Edge To CiviCRM To Zoho, And The Data That Left, 2004–2026 · Version 3

How the Libertarian National Committee left Raiser’s Edge, built and abandoned CiviCRM, moved to Zoho, sold the CiviCRM system to Ken Moellman, and lost track of who held its member data. This version adds what each person later said about those events, set against the record.
